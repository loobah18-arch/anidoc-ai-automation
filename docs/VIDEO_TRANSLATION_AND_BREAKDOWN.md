## 2. Complete Step-by-Step Translation of the Workflow

### Stage 1: Topic Selection (The 10-Topic Viral Filter)
- The pipeline begins by filtering documentary ideas into 5 proven high-retention buckets:
  1. **Historical Crime & Underworld**: Mumbai mafia syndicates, Dawood Ibrahim, Haji Mastan, D-Company secrets.
  2. **Espionage & Secret Operations**: RAW (Research and Analysis Wing) covert operations, IB intelligence, 1971 Indo-Pak war pilot jailbreaks, Operation Sindoor, Mossad/CIA operations.
  3. **Political History & Power**: Emergency period, high-stakes parliamentary confrontations, Indira Gandhi assassination, geopolitical crises.
  4. **Untold Real Events**: Bank heists, airline hijackings, mysterious disappearances, royal family secrets.
  5. **Fresh Verified News (Last 30-60 Days)**: Recent investigative events backed by official court records or government inquiries.
- **Key Principle**: Never produce unverified conspiracies about sitting leaders; focus on documented facts, published biographies, declassified files, and court records.

---

### Stage 2: Style DNA Analysis & Deep Cinematic Scriptwriting
Chad Grow breaks down the **AniDoc Scriptwriting Formula**:
- **Target Word Count**: 3,000 to 4,500 Hindi (or English) words, perfectly calibrated for a 10–15 minute video.
- **The Hook (First 30 Seconds)**:
  - Must open mid-action with a precise date, city/location, and sensory detail in the first 3 sentences.
  - Immediately poses a question or high-stakes dilemma without revealing the answer.
- **Sentence Rhythm**:
  - Short, punchy sentences (maximum 2 clauses per sentence).
  - Alternates rapid narrative bursts with slow dramatic pauses.
- **Retention Engineering**:
  - Injects a new escalation, revelation, or twist every 90–120 seconds.
  - Marks peak emotional moments with `[TENSION PEAK]`.
- **Tone**:
  - Third-person cinematic narrator. Authoritative, serious, grave, and restrained—never cartoonish, hyperactive, or sensationalist.
- **Clean Voiceover Prose**:
  - Free of markdown headings, narrator labels, sound effect brackets `[MUSIC]`, or scene tags so the text can be copied directly into Text-to-Speech engines.
- **Duration Meter**:
  - Calculates video length at 50 WPM (slow/dramatic: ~60-80 min), 100 WPM (normal documentary pace: 30-45 min or ~15 min for 1,500-word blocks), and 160 WPM (fast pace).

---

### Stage 3: Visual Style DNA & Batch Image Prompts
To mimic the signature AniDoc visual aesthetic:
- **Art Style**: Semi-realistic 2D illustration with clean vector outlines, no noisy textures, accurate human anatomy, and South Asian character features.
- **Color Palette**: Warm muted tones—amber, khaki, dusty olive, deep navy, charcoal black. Skin tones in warm brown/golden. Backgrounds slightly desaturated.
- **Lighting**: Single-source dramatic lighting—amber streetlamp, moonlight through a window, overhead interrogation lamp, strong side-light casting deep cinematic shadows.
- **Composition & Camera**:
  - Medium-close portrait shots (chest up) for emotional character drama.
  - Wide establishing shots for environments and historical settings.
  - Slight low-angle shots for powerful figures.
- **16:9 Mandatory**: Every prompt explicitly specifies 16:9 aspect ratio.
- **Batching**: Generated in batches of 20 prompts inside clean code blocks. Every prompt is 100% standalone and describes subject, attire, environment, props, lighting, camera angle, and mood.

---

### Stage 4: Video Motion Prompts
To convert 2D static illustrations into animated documentary footage:
- Prompts are generated for AI video generators (Flow AI / Google Veo 3.1 Fast, Kling AI, Runway Gen-3, Luma Dream Machine).
- **Subtle Camera Movements**:
  - Slow push-in (slow zoom-in on character's eyes for tension).
  - Gentle horizontal pan (across a war map or cabinet meeting room).
  - Atmospheric environmental dynamics: drifting smoke, falling rain, street dust, flicker of amber lamps.
  - 3 to 5 second duration per shot.

---

### Stage 5: Viral High-CTR Thumbnail Architecture
Chad Grow shows the 5-point thumbnail design formula:
1. **Devanagari Bold Headline**: Large Devanagari Hindi text at top center (White text with 1 high-voltage power keyword highlighted in Red or Yellow, e.g., 'खौफ', 'धोखा', 'रहस्य', 'सीक्रेट').
2. **Split Composition**: Left side dark/dramatic (red smoke, rain, night) vs Right side contextual setting (parliament, map, bunker).
3. **Character Prominence**: 1–2 large illustrated figures (chest up) with intense, shocked, or calculating facial expressions.
4. **Contrast**: Near-black / deep navy background against bright character highlights.
5. **The Signature Badge**: Yellow rounded badge at bottom center with bold black text: **`2D ANIMATION`**.

---

### Stage 6: Complete YouTube SEO & Growth Package
- **5 Title Variations**: Curiosity gap, shocking revelation, investigative "How/Why", viral hook, and punchy short title.
- **Description**: 150–200 word keyword-dense summary in documentary tone with timestamps and CTA.
- **15 Viral Hashtags**: High search volume niche tags.
- **30 YouTube Tags**: Optimized mix of Hindi and English search queries.
