## 1. Unit Economics & RPM Breakdown

Documentary and true crime/history channels enjoy significantly higher CPM/RPM rates than gaming or entertainment niches because:
- **Long Video Durations (10–20 minutes)**: Allows multiple mid-roll ad placements (e.g., at 3m, 6m, 9m, 12m).
- **High Audience Retention (50–65%)**: Curiosity-gap storytelling keeps viewers watching till the end.
- **Older Demographic (18–45 males)**: Highly valued by advertisers in finance, tech, education, security, and VPN sectors.

## 2. Multi-Channel Revenue Streams

### A. YouTube AdSense (Primary)
- Enable mid-roll ads every 3.5 minutes.
- Optimize thumbnail CTR (aim for 9% to 14% CTR).
- Target Tier-1 NRI audiences (US, UK, UAE, Canada) alongside Indian viewership to boost average RPM.

### B. Brand Sponsorships (Brand Deals)
Top sponsors for documentary channels include:
- **Cybersecurity & VPNs**: NordVPN, Surfshark, ExpressVPN ($1,000–$3,000 per integration).
- **Trading & Investment Apps**: Zerodha, Angel One, TradingView.
- **Audio & Book Apps**: Audible, KukuFM, PocketFM.

### C. Affiliate Links in Description
- Hardware / Security tools.
- Books referenced in the documentary (Amazon Affiliate links).
- AI tools & software affiliate programs.

## 3. High-Velocity Production Schedule

To scale rapidly to the YouTube algorithm's recommendation engine:
- **Week 1–4**: Publish 2 long-form documentary videos per week (8 videos total).
- **Week 5–8**: Extract 3 high-tension 60-second YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels from each documentary to drive massive top-of-funnel traffic to the full video.
- **Consistency Rule**: Never miss upload dates. Use `publishers/youtube_publisher.py` to schedule 2 weeks of content in advance.
