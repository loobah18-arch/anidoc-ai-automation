## ■ ANIDOC STYLE DNA

- **Niche**: True crime, espionage, political history, untold real events — investigative documentary style.
- **Target Audience**: Viewers aged 18–45 interested in real power, crime, politics, and national security.
- **Hook Style**: Opens mid-action with a precise date, city, and sensory detail within first 3 sentences. Immediately raises unanswered stakes.
- **Sentence Rhythm**: Short punchy sentences. Max 2 clauses per sentence. Alternates fast narrative bursts with slow dramatic pauses.
- **Tone**: Third-person cinematic narrator. Controlled gravity. Authoritative, serious, never sensational.
- **Word Count**: 3,000–4,500 words per script (10–15 min video).
- **Curiosity Gaps**: Raises a question, withholds answer for 60–90 seconds, then delivers with maximum impact.
- **Retention**: Injects a new escalation, revelation, or twist every 90–120 seconds. Marks tension peaks with `[TENSION PEAK]`.
- **Language**: Formal Hindi with Urdu flavor for crime/spy topics (or crisp formal English documentary prose). Avoids casual Hinglish.

## ■ VISUAL STYLE PROFILE

- **Art Style**: Semi-realistic 2D illustration. Clean outlines, no texture noise. Accurate human anatomy, detailed expressive South Asian features.
- **Color Palette**: Warm muted tones: amber, khaki, dusty olive, deep navy, charcoal black. Skin tones: warm brown/golden. Backgrounds desaturated. Single dramatic light accent (amber lamp, blue moonlight).
- **Lighting**: Single-source dramatic lighting — strong side-light or overhead spotlight. Deep shadows. High cinematic contrast.
- **Camera Style**: Medium-close portrait shots (chest up) for character scenes. Wide establishing shots for environments. Slight low-angle for powerful figures.
- **Composition**: Subject fills 60–70% of frame. Shallow background detail.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 — MANDATORY on every image prompt.
- **Real Figures**: Historical/deceased figures rendered in full likeness; currently serving heads of state rendered symbolically (silhouettes, settings, maps).

## ■ THUMBNAIL STYLE PROFILE

- **Headline Text**: Bold Hindi in Devanagari (or bold English). White main text + 1 keyword highlighted in RED or YELLOW. ExtraBold font. Large — fills top 25–35% of thumbnail.
- **Text Placement**: Headline top-center or top-full-width. `'2D ANIMATION'` badge bottom-center in yellow rounded rectangle.
- **Characters**: 1–2 main illustrated figures, large, center/right. Intense, grave, or calculating facial expression.
- **Background**: Split composition — one side dark dramatic scene (red smoke, rain, night), other side context (parliament, map, building).
- **Color Contrast**: Dark background vs bright character highlight.
