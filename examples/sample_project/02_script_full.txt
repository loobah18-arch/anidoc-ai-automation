13 August 1972. Rawalpindi Central Jail ki High-Security Barrack Number 4. Raat ke 2 bajkar 15 minute. Bahar musladhar barish ki aawaz aur jail ki boundary par ghoomti searchlight ka peela ujala. Ek geeli kothri ki farsh ke neeche, lohe ki ek choti si pattar se teen Bhartiya yudh-bandi mitti khod rahe the. Flight Lieutenant Dilip Parulkar, Flight Lieutenant Malvinder Singh Grewal aur Flying Officer Harish Sinhji. Teen mahino se in teeno afsaro ne ek chammach aur ek tootey hue lohe ke rod se deewar ke neeche 12 foot lambi surang khodi thi. Unhe pata tha ki agar wo pakde gaye, toh court martial ya seedhe goli unka intezar kar rahi thi.
